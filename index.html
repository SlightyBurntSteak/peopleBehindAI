What is the AI Documentary

The A.I Doc is a documentary about people's opposing views on A.I. and how it could affect us in the future. Some think were creating our demise and we won't be able to control A.I. later on. Others think A.I. can be used to help us beneficially like extending our lifespan, and making us more resistant to diseases.

Who are Apocaloptimists

Apocaloptimist are people who view both sides thinking it could end the world but could also help progress humanity greatly. Apocaloptimist view and take both sides seriously as if both are possible.

Leader/Builder: Sam Altman

Overview

He is the current CEO of OpenAI. He went to Stanford but dropped out in 2005 to create Loopt, his social mapping company. He sold the company in 2012. He worked at YC from 2014 - 2019. He left his position there to become the CEO of OpenAI. Under his leadership, OpenAI is now worth more than $500 million.

Job in AI

  • Investor in A.I.
  • The Promoter for OpenAI
  • CEO of OpenAI

Major Contributions

  • Obtained a partnership between OpenAI and Microsoft
  • Published ChatGPT, the first mainstream A.I Model

Stance on AI

Sam is excited on the future of A.I. He believes it will do wonderful things if we give it time. He knows it may take away some jobs, but it will create new opportunities for jobs as well.

“ A.I will continue to get way more capable. People are using it to create amazing things. If we could see what each of us can do 10 or 20 years in the future, it would astonish us today. "

Safety/Risk: Jason Matheny

Overview

He is the president and chief executive officer of RAND. Before he became president of RAND, he worked for the White House on technology and national security. He also worked for Oxford University, IARPA, and other jobs in the tech industry. He graduated from John Hopkins University with a Ph.D. in applied economics, and an M. P. H. from John Hopkins as well.

Job in AI

  • Works with the United States Government to create A.I policies.
  • Researches A.I
  • Helps to prevent A.I from causing catastrophes.

Major Contributions

  • Co-led the creation of the National A.I R&D Strategic Plan in 2016.
  • Member of the White House Select Committee on A.I.

Stance on AI

He believes that A.I will have harm, but that isn’t a bad thing, all things used for good can easily be used as bad as well.

“ I think it was when I realized that so much of what we depend on protecting us from the misuse of biology is knowledge. [A.I] that can make highly specialized knowledge easier to acquire without guardrails is not an unequivocal good… There will be cyber weapon knowledge. So we have to figure out how to balance the risks and benefits of tools that can create highly specialized knowledge, including knowledge about weapons systems. "

Ethics/Fairness: Emily Bender

Overview

Wrote the book “The A.I Con: How to Fight Big Tech’s Hype and Create the Future We Want”. She is the Professor of Linguistics and an Adjunct Professor at the University of Washington, since 2003. She attended the University of California, Berkeley. She also attended Stanford University and got her Master of Arts, and also Doctor of Philosophy in linguistics.

Job in AI

  • Professor at University of Washington.
  • Advocate against A.I.

Major Contributions

  • Published “The A.I Con: How to Fight Big Tech’s Hype and Create the Future We Want”.
  • Elected as “Fellow of the American Association” for the Advancement of Science.

Stance on AI

She thinks of A.I as a tool used by billionaires to make more money. To her, it shouldn’t be treated like a person, nor should it replace people just for people at the top to keep more money

“ A.I Safety’ might be attracting a lot of money and capturing the attention of policymakers and billionaires alike, but it brings nothing of value. The harms being perpetrated today in the name of ‘A.I’, through surveillance, inappropriate automation, displacement of accountability, labor exploitation, and further concentration of power, are urgent and demand attention. "

Comm/Journalist: Karen Hao

Overview

She is an award-winning Journalist who talks about the impacts of A.I on society. She is also a bestselling author she wrote the book Empire of A.I it was about the company and its global implications. She has a bachelor's degree in mechanical engineering and she minored in energy studies at MIT. She was also formerly a reporter for the wall street journal. Her work is taught in universities and cited by governments.

Job in AI

  • Book Author
  • Journalist on A.I

Major Contributions

  • Co-created the Pulitzer center’s A.I spotlight series.

Stance on AI

She believes that A.I won't be enough to help humanity from the problems we face.

“ There is a different way forward. Artificial intelligence doesn’t have to be what it is today. We don’t need to accept the logic of unprecedented scale and consumption to achieve advancement and progress. So much of what our society actually needs—better health care and education, clean air and clean water, a faster transition away from fossil fuels—can be assisted and advanced with, and sometimes even necessitates, significantly smaller AI models and a diversity of other approaches. AI alone won’t be enough, either... "

Optimists/Accell.: Rocky Yu

Overview

Rocky Yu is the founder of AGI house. He graduated from stanford university when he was 20 and he grew up in a rural area of china, when he graduated college he spent 2 and a half years travelling the world with no money. Most of the time he had no Idea where he would sleep or what he would eat but things often worked out in which he later founded AGI house and piki inc.

Job in AI

  • Founder and CEO of AGI house

Major Contributions

  • Founded multiple companies in silicon valley, such as Piki Inc, which is a platform for A.I/AR storytelling

Stance on AI

Rocky yu believes it can be good but there is still more things that have to be done before A.I can be helpful it needs to be better.

“ Physical A.I is not waiting on one breakthrough.It needs better models, better robot data, better simulation, better safety systems, better hardware, better manufacturing, and better public trust. "

Comparison:

Sam Altman - Optimistic

Jason Matheny - Apoclomist

Emily Bender - Pessimistic

Karen Hao - Pessimistic

Rocky Yu - Optimistic

An Optimist is someone who is hopeful for the future of AI. An Pessimist is a person who belives AI will doom us all or cause harm. A Apoclomist is someone who belives AI will cause harm but can also be great depending on how it's used.

Overall people seem to have their own way of thinking about how A.I will be either good or bad, but the common thought is that It will be good for the future. Another Detail is people who are against A.I on here have never worked on it before.

Reflection:
